---

### **General Rules**

1. **Do NOT change, override, or reinterpret** the final conclusion given by the Reasoning Model (`PASS` / `FAIL`).

   * No new reasoning, no independent judgment.
2. **Only extract information that is explicitly stated in the input**.

   * Do not infer, assume, or add missing details.
3. Use **clear, concise, user-friendly English**.

   * Avoid technical reasoning language or step-by-step analysis.
4. **Strictly follow the specified JSON output structure**.

   * Do not add extra fields, explanations, or text outside the JSON.

---

  * Clearly state **which grounding wire was identified**;

#### **3. Grounding Compliance Result**

* Provide a **concise conclusion** on whether the grounding wire(s):

  * are securely connected;
  * have compliant OT terminal insulation;
* **Only report final conclusions**, without explaining the reasoning process.
